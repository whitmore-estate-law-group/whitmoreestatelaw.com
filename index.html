Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a will and a trust?

A will provides instructions for distributing property after death. A trust can hold and manage property under its terms.

How long does probate take?

The timeline depends on the estate, court requirements, and other circumstances.

Can I update my estate plan?

Estate planning documents can often be changed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

What are digital assets?

They may include cryptocurrency, online accounts, and other forms of digital property.
