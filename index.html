Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a will and a trust?

A will directs distribution of assets at death; a trust can hold and manage assets under its terms.

How long does probate take?

Timing varies with the estate and applicable Illinois procedures.

Can I update my estate plan later?

Estate documents can often be updated subject to applicable legal requirements.

What are digital assets?

They can include cryptocurrency, online accounts, and other digitally held property.
